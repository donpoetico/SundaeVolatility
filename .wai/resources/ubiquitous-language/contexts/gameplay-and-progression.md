# Gameplay and Progression

This context covers campaign structure, unlocks, deal flow, customers, and outcomes.

## Canonical Terms

| Canonical term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| campaign | A full run to Day 90 |
| day | One top-level unit of play |
| phase | Morning, Midday, or Evening |
| walk-in customer | A spot-sale customer generated by demand |
| named character | A recurring counterparty with authored behavior |
| deal | A negotiable contract opportunity before acceptance |
| deal slip | The tangible in-world record of a proposed or active deal |
| position | The active commitment created after a deal is accepted |
| contract | The formal agreement represented by a deal slip and, once accepted, a position |
| standing order | Auto-accept rule for matching deals |
| milestone | A threshold that unlocks content |
| unlock | A newly available flavor, room, tool, or system |
| settlement | End-of-life resolution for a contract |
| shortfall penalty | Penalty for failing to fulfill a forward |
| outcome tier | End-of-campaign result band |

## Contract Taxonomy

| Canonical term | Meaning | Typical counterpart |
|---|---|---|
| forward contract | Future delivery at agreed price | Mrs. Chen |
| call option | Right to buy later | Dmitri |
| put option | Right to sell later / guaranteed floor | Agnes |
| compound deal | One logical deal with multiple linked slips or terms | Advanced play |

## Progression Semantics

- **unlock** means the player can now access or purchase something.
- **reveal** means something becomes visible in the world.
- **renovation** means a shop-space upgrade that changes available interactions.
- **mastery** refers to advanced understanding and late-game capability, not just high balance.

## Economy Vocabulary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| register balance | Current spendable cash |
| revenue | Gross money brought in |
| profit/loss | Net result over a period or deal |
| capital allocation | Choice of where money goes: inventory, promises, or knowledge/tools |
| knowledge costs money | Core pacing principle: learning tools compete with trading capital |

## Behavioral Vocabulary

| Canonical term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| risk tolerance | How much uncertainty a character accepts |
| price sensitivity | How strongly a character reacts to repricing |
| information quality | How good a character's market signal is |
| max counter-offer delta | Largest tolerated repricing from the initial proposal |
| patience window | Time before a counterparty withdraws |

## Counterparty Vocabulary

| Character | Canonical role language |
|---|---|
| Mrs. Chen | forward counterparty, bulk buyer, fulfillment-sensitive |
| Dmitri | call-option counterparty, upside seeker |
| Agnes | put-option counterparty, floor-price guarantor |
| Zosia | information-rich counterparty, signal-driven negotiator |

## Distinctions to Preserve

- **walk-in customer** is not the same as a **named character**.
- **deal** is the negotiation opportunity; **position** is the resulting active commitment.
- **deal slip** is the physical artifact; **contract** is the agreement it represents.
- **milestone** is a rule threshold; **goal** is a player objective.
- **failure** as an outcome tier does not mean the run taught nothing; knowledge persists as part of the game's educational arc.
