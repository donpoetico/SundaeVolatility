## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Behavioral Fidelity First
The interface SHALL teach primarily through consequences in play rather than explicit explanation. Player-facing presentation SHALL let a player form and test hypotheses about promises using weather, customer reactions, stock condition, slips, and tools without requiring technical vocabulary or always-visible numerical overlays.

#### Scenario: Learn from consequences without jargon
- **WHEN** a player spends several days accepting similar promises under changing weather
- **THEN** they can notice stable patterns and meaningful contrasts through artifact behavior, customer reactions, and resolution outcomes without being shown finance jargon.

### Requirement: Layered Proof Model
The interface SHALL present proof of the financial core in layers. The default layer SHALL be diegetic and artifact-based, using slips, tools, calculator tickets, archive outcomes, and journal notes. A deeper explicit audit layer SHALL exist as an optional second step for advanced inspection.

#### Scenario: Stay in artifact-first mode
- **WHEN** a player evaluates and resolves promises during normal play
- **THEN** they can understand why a decision felt sound or shaky by comparing artifacts and outcomes without opening any technical panel.

#### Scenario: Open the deeper proof layer
- **WHEN** an advanced player asks to inspect how a promise was judged more explicitly
- **THEN** they can move from the diegetic artifact to a deeper audit view rather than bypassing the artifact layer entirely.

### Requirement: Grandfather's Method Framing
The optional explicit audit layer SHALL be framed in-world as Grandfather's method rather than as a detached developer console. The framing SHALL make advanced inspection feel like deeper access to the same truth the rest of the game already expresses.

#### Scenario: Discover Grandfather's method
- **WHEN** the player reaches the point where deeper inspection becomes available
- **THEN** the game presents it as a discovered judging method tied to Grandfather's desk, notes, or instruments, not as a separate meta UI.
