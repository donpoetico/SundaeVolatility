## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Resolved Promise Archive Band
The corkboard SHALL include a nearby resolved archive band that preserves compact memory of completed promises. Archived items SHALL retain their visual identity, a small context cue, and an outcome cue while occupying less space than active promises.

#### Scenario: Move a resolved promise into memory
- **WHEN** a promise resolves
- **THEN** its slip leaves the active grid and appears in the archive band as a compact version preserving promise type, flavor, a day or weather cue, and outcome identity.

### Requirement: Judgment-Weighted Resolution Feedback
Promise resolution SHALL communicate both lived result and quality of judgment. The default feedback SHALL use a tactile ritual on the slip or board, and archive outcomes SHALL be weighted more toward whether the choice was reasonable under the conditions than toward raw luck alone.

#### Scenario: Sound judgment, mixed result
- **WHEN** a player makes a reasonable decision under uncertain conditions and the outcome is still disappointing
- **THEN** the resolution ritual and archive marking communicate a costly result without implying the player necessarily judged the promise poorly.

### Requirement: Archive Outcome Scanability
Archived promises SHALL use both a compact stamped phrase and strong visual/material treatment so players can scan outcomes quickly without reading long text. The default archive view SHALL support sub-second comparison across several past promises.

#### Scenario: Compare several archived promises quickly
- **WHEN** the player glances across the archive band
- **THEN** they can distinguish outcome quality through stamp wording, paper wear, and color/material cues before leaning in for more detail.

### Requirement: Reactive Artifact and Journal Notes
The game SHALL support graduated reactive notes that help players notice patterns without replacing discovery. Early hints SHALL appear as tiny local annotations on slips, tickets, or margins; fuller journal reflections SHALL appear only after a pattern becomes established in play history.

#### Scenario: Early nudge after repeated misread
- **WHEN** the player repeats a similar misjudgment a small number of times
- **THEN** a tiny annotation appears on the relevant artifact, hinting that the pattern deserves attention without explicitly lecturing.

#### Scenario: Fuller reflection after established pattern
- **WHEN** the player's history establishes a recurring pattern across several days
- **THEN** a more complete journal note appears, connecting several prior cases in Grandfather's voice.
