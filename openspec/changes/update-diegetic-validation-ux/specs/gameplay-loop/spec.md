## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Learn-By-Doing Pattern Arc
The gameplay loop SHALL teach through a progression of pattern recognition: repeated patterns first, then richer contrasts, then optional curiosity-driven inspection. Early play SHALL favor a small action space and strong consequence clarity so players can form hypotheses without tutorial-heavy explanation.

#### Scenario: Stable pattern before deeper complexity
- **WHEN** the player encounters an early family of similar promises across several days
- **THEN** the game surfaces enough repeated cues and outcomes for the player to recognize the pattern before introducing more complex contrasts.

### Requirement: Corkboard as Learning Memory
The gameplay loop SHALL treat the corkboard as both an active commitment board and the player's memory system for judgment. Daily signals from the shop SHALL be comparable against both active promises and nearby archived outcomes.

#### Scenario: Compare today against prior cases
- **WHEN** the player leans into the corkboard while considering a new promise
- **THEN** they can compare the current case against active slips and nearby archived evidence rather than relying on memory alone.

### Requirement: Selective Comparison Cues
The gameplay loop SHALL occasionally surface terse comparison cues when a contrast is especially educational. These cues SHALL appear only when they reveal a player-learning moment or a meaningful difference between superficially similar promises.

#### Scenario: Highlight a meaningful contrast
- **WHEN** two similar-looking promises behave differently because conditions differed in an important way
- **THEN** the game surfaces a short comparison cue through artifacts or the journal, helping the player notice the distinction.

#### Scenario: Reinforce a player-learning moment
- **WHEN** the player's recent judgments show a clear improvement or recurring blind spot
- **THEN** the game surfaces a short comparison cue that frames the moment as a pattern worth noticing.
